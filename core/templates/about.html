The DriveHub Standard

Engineered For Excellence

DriveHub is a luxury automotive car rental management platform designed to unify vehicle selection, rule-based Indian driving licence verification, and digital pre/post vehicle inspections into a single seamless dashboard.

Premium & Normal Fleets

Choose from economical daily commuters (Toyota, Hyundai, Honda) or elevate your journey with high-performance luxury sports models (BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Porsche, Tesla).

Rule-Based DL Verification

Security and road compliance are guaranteed. Customers submit Indian driving licences for Parivahan Sarathi RTO verification, with automated age thresholds and expiry checks before any rental booking is allowed.

Digital Inspections

Complete transparent vehicle audits before handover and upon return. We record odometer readings, fuel gauges, interior cleanliness, and automated damage assessment reports in Indian Rupees (₹).

Driving Licence & Eligibility Policy

To rent vehicles on DriveHub, customers must satisfy the project's rule-based criteria:

Normal Category Age: 21+ Years

  • Valid Indian Driving Licence (LMV - Light Motor Vehicle) from State RTO / Parivahan.
  • DigiLocker / mParivahan digital DL or Smart Card accepted.
  • Non-expired licence verified by administrator.
  • Refundable security deposit (₹3,000).

Premium Category Age: 25+ Years

  • Permanent Indian Driving Licence with verified credentials.
  • Minimum 25 years old at date of booking.
  • Identity proof & verified customer profile.
  • Refundable luxury security deposit (₹10,000).
